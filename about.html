Mlis Restaurent

1.PHNOM PENH

Malis serves Living Cambodian Cuisine in a tranquil courtyard with flowing fish pond,
greeneries & intricate architecture surrounded by indoor seating. Open for breakfast,
lunch and dinner, Malis also offers private dining rooms for business lunch or special events.

2.SIEM REAP

Located in the heart of Siem Reap, just minutes from the iconic Angkor Wat,Malis Siem Reap
is a culinary gem that celebrates Nouvelle's Cambodian Cuisine. Its peaceful terrace, surrounded
by lush greenery, offers a tranquil setting to enjoy the rich flavors of traditional Cambodian dishes.

3.KTI Airport

Located at Techo International Airport, Malis KTI is a culinary gem celebrating Express Living
Cambodian Cuisine. While awaiting your flight, indulge in the rich flavors of authentic Cambodian
dishes, thoughtfully crafted to reflect the nation's heritage.
